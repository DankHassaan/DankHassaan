## About Me

I am a cybersecurity graduate (Bachelor of Cybersecurity, ACS Endorsed) from La Trobe University. During a SOC Analyst internship at One Step Group, a managed security service provider, I triaged and investigated alerts in Microsoft Sentinel across 25+ client environments, wrote KQL queries to detect anomalous activity, and built an Identity and Access Management workbook.

I chose cybersecurity because I feel a real sense of responsibility to contribute to a fast growing industry that supports the world economy. I also have a protective nature, and I want to be there when organisations need to be defended.

I am currently studying for Microsoft SC-200 and Blue Team Level 1 (BTL1) & Blue Team Junior Analyst (BTJA) by Centri, and I keep building my skills with TryHackMe labs, CTF challenges and Docker based home labs. I am looking for a junior or Level 1 SOC Analyst role and I am available for 24/7 rostered work.
